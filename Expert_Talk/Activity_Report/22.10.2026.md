## 2. Speaker Profile

Dr. Rajesh Kumar is a Professor and Head of the Department of Civil Engineering at Chandigarh College of Engineering and Technology (CCET), Chandigarh. His academic and professional experience in civil engineering provided a valuable foundation for sharing insights into the principles and applications of self-compacting concrete.

## 3. Report on the Activity

The Department of Civil Engineering, Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College (GNDEC), Ludhiana, organised an expert lecture on **“Principles of Self-Compacting Concrete”** on 22 October 2026 at the TCC Seminar Hall. The session was delivered by Dr. Rajesh Kumar, Professor and Head, Department of Civil Engineering, CCET, Chandigarh.

The lecture focused on the principles of self-compacting concrete (SCC), a specialised concrete that can flow and consolidate under its own weight without requiring mechanical vibration. The session provided an opportunity for students and faculty members to strengthen their understanding of this important development in concrete technology and its relevance to modern construction practices.

The programme was conducted under the mentorship of **Dr. Harvinder Singh, Professor and Head, Department of Civil Engineering, GNDEC**, reflecting the department's continued emphasis on expert interaction and the integration of academic learning with professional knowledge. Dr. Yuvraj Singh coordinated the event on behalf of the department.

## 4. Outcome of the Activity

- Enhanced awareness of the fundamental principles and characteristics of self-compacting concrete.
- Strengthened understanding of the relevance of SCC in modern concrete construction.
- Provided an opportunity for students and faculty members to interact with an experienced academician from another technical institution.
- Encouraged further learning about advances in concrete technology and their practical applications.
