## 2. Speaker Profile

**Er. Awtar Koonar** is a distinguished alumnus of Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, having graduated in Electrical Engineering in 1971. He has over 30 years of professional experience in project and programme management, systems engineering, and disaster management.

## 3. Report on the Activity

An expert lecture on **“Disaster & Project Management”** was organized at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College (GNDEC), Ludhiana, on 21 October 2026 at the TCC Seminar Hall. The session featured Er. Awtar Koonar, a distinguished alumnus of the institution, who shared insights from his extensive professional experience in project and programme management, systems engineering, and disaster management.

The lecture highlighted the importance of systematic project planning, effective management practices, and data-driven decision-making in addressing complex engineering and disaster-management challenges. The speaker also discussed the growing relevance of **Geographic Information Systems (GIS), data analytics, and Artificial Intelligence (AI)** in contemporary professional workflows. Emphasis was placed on the importance of reliable data, data interpretation, and the integration of emerging technologies for informed decision-making.

The session offered valuable perspectives on the practical applications of these technologies and their relevance to engineering education and professional practice.

**Dr. Sehijpal Singh Khangura, Principal, GNDEC**, appreciated the expert's interaction with the students and faculty members and emphasized the importance of connecting academic learning with professional experience.

The event was coordinated by **Dr. Yuvraj Singh, Department of Civil Engineering**, who extended a vote of thanks to the speaker, the college leadership, faculty members, and students for their participation and support.

The lecture concluded with an emphasis on the role of effective project management, technological advancement, and data-driven approaches in addressing contemporary engineering and disaster-management challenges.

---

## 4. Outcome of the Activity

The expert lecture provided exposure to contemporary approaches in disaster and project management, with particular emphasis on the application of GIS, data analytics, and AI. It also highlighted the relevance of interdisciplinary knowledge and practical experience in preparing future engineers to address complex real-world challenges.
